Occupational Safety, Sanitation, School Health and Fire Prevention

TABLE OF CONTENTS

  • 1. Abbreviations
  • 2. General provisions
  • 3. Detail provisions

You can check the Vietnamese version in the PDF Tab.

1. Abbreviations

  • OSH: Occupational safety and health
  • SAM: Student Affairs Management
  • OP: Operation Department

2. General provisionsTop of page

2.1. All employees, students, customers, partners who are working, or coming to work, visiting, studying must comply with regulations on OSH, Sanitation, Fire Prevention & Control and follow the instructions of Operation Department.

2.2. The Operation Department is responsible for the implementation of OSH, Sanitation, Firefighting & Rescue activities in accordance with this regulation and current legal regulations.

2.3. The SAM Department is responsible for arranging enough medical staff or signing a contract with a qualified medical facility to organize a medical room, equipping, and inspecting medical facilities to comply with regulations.

3. Detail provisionsTop of page

3.1. Prepare, update, archive legal documents and plans on OSH, Sanitation, School health, Fire Prevention, Fighting & Rescue:

    • Operation department prepares, archives and updates legal documents and plans on OSH, Sanitation, Fire prevention, fire fighting & rescue in accordance with the Law on OSH, the Law on Environmental Protection, the Law on Fire Protection, and the guiding documents.
    • SAM department plans and implements school health works according to circular 33/2021/TT-BYT

3.2. Training activities:

    • Operation department is responsible for establishing the fire prevention and fighting team; organizing training and issuing professional certificates on fire fighting and rescue teams; practicing fire fighting and rescue plans according to Decree 136/2020/ND-CP, Decree 83/2017/ND-CP, Circular 149/2020/TT-BCA, Circular 08/2018/TT- BCA.
      Operation Department organizes training courses to issue OSH cards and certificates for employees according to Decree 44/2016/ND-CP, Decree 140/2018/ND-CP and Circular 06 2020/TT- BLDTBXH.
    • Operation Department guides the regulations and measures to ensure OSH, Sanitation and Fire Protection for employees, students, and contractors.
    • SAM Department is responsible for organizing propaganda on the work of school health and disease prevention

3.3. Inspection activities:

    • Operation Department develops and organizes the implementation of the annual plan on OSH, Fire Prevention, Fighting and Rescue according to the contents in Annexes I and II attached current regulations, and also takes the main role of inspection activities by Government agency on OSH, Fire Prevention, Fighting and Rescue; take the initiative to make plans and measures to overcome the shortcomings after the inspection.
    • SAM Department develops and organizes the implementation of the plan and periodically self-checks the work of school health in accordance with Appendix II; and also takes the main role of inspection activities by Government agency on school health; take the initiative to make plans and measures to overcome the shortcomings after the inspection.

3.4. Statistics and reports:

    • Operation Department conducts investigation, assessment, declaration and announcement of occupational accidents and diseases for employees and competent authorities.
    • Operation Department makes inspection declaration for equipment with strict requirements on OSH; Reports periodically to the authorities about occupational accidents; occupational safety and health; environmental protection work; periodic fire prevention and fighting work according to regulations or at the request of authorities.
    • SAM Department reports on the work of school health periodically according to regulation

4. When the legal documents referenced in this Regulation are amended, supplemented, or replaced, the contents referred in this Regulation shall also be adjusted and implemented in accordance with the amended, supplemented, or replaced.

No. Categories Frequency Contents Responsible

  • Safety pin and seal are available, the nozzle is not cracked or broken
  • Powder tank: Indicator needle is green (works well)
  • CO2 tank: Enough weight according to manufacture specifications.
 Technician Officer

  • Cabinet is not broken, deformed. The door does not sag, lose hinges/latches
  • Medical tools and rescue tools are in good condition.
 Technician Officer
3 Fire escape staircase 01 month/times
  • Hydraulic auto door-closer works well
  • Floor indicator board is not peeling.
  • Stair handrails are not cracked or broken.
  • No obstacles, ensure convenient to move and escape
  • Exit stairs door handles work well
 Technician Officer
4 Emergency exit lamp and exit route 01 month/times
  • Exit lamp: Right direction, no deviate, no sag, adequate lighting time.
  • Emergency exit door, emergency exit route is not blocked
 Technician Officer
5 Emergency lighting 01 month/times
  • Emergency lighting: No deviate, no sag, adequate lighting time and must be discharged quarterly
 Technician Officer
6 Firefighting pumping system 01 week/times
  • Pump control switch in auto status, no leak water and no abnormal sound when running test.
  • All equipment are in normal operation status
  • Level of fire water tank is at least 80%
 Technician Officer
7 Automatic fire alarm system 01 month/times
  • Fire alarm center: Display is clear, function keys are clear, the operation is stable, backup battery works well.
  • Fire bell, fire alarm button: Operate well when testing
  • Fire detector: Activate time no more than 120 seconds for heat detectors and no more than 30 seconds for smoke detectors.
  • Interlocking devices include Magnetic doors, elevators, smoke extraction systems, pressure booster systems, fire stop valves, sound systems… operate according to the fire fighting scenario.
  • Residual pressure in staircase chamber: 20 – 50 Pascal
 Technician Officer
8 Indoor fire hose system 03 months/times
  • Angle hydrant valve: Hand screw firm, no loss bolt, no rust, no leak water.
  • Indoor fire hose cabinets: No deformed, no peeling paint, no breaking glass, no lock, hinges and latches no damage.
  • Fire hose reel: The nozzles have rubber gaskets, easy to disassemble.
  • Spray nozzle, coupling: Works well
 Technician Officer
9 Fire hydrant 03 months/times
  • The upper barrel no leak water, hose outlet is easy to operate
  • The valve and the barrel are not cracked, broken, or deformed
 Technician Officer
10 Sprinkler, Drencher system 03 months/times
  • The system control valves are easy to operate, no rust, no leak water, have open/close tags.
  • Fire sprinklers are not covered, obstructed, dry, do not leak water, do not corrode
  • Control valves of Drencher are operated when testing 02 fire detectors of different channel or 02 address fire detectors.
 Technician Officer

 Inert gas fire system (FM200, …) 03 months/times

  • Distribution cabinets must have insulated plate between the hot

 Technician Officer
16 LGP storage and usage area 01 week/times
  • Operating instruction and emergency contact numbers are available.
  • LPG volume is according to fire prevention and fighting approval or maximum 70kg.
  • Gas leak detector installed no more than 30cm above the ground and unobstructed, work well.
  • Safety warning signs are available: no fire, no smoke, no telephone
  • Fire fighting equipments are available
  • LPG usage operators are trained and have professional certificates in fire prevention and fighting; Understand valve diagram and gas valve opening/closing operation
 Technician Officer
17 Tele- communication station 03 month/times
  • Facility system is according to the approved design, install more without permission is not allowed.
  • Electrical system is properly connected and grounded, not generate heat.
  • The ventilation and cooling system ensure the temperature from 20÷25ºC, the air conditioner operates alternately.
  • Fire alarm and fire fighting equipment is in good working condition as designed, not covered.
  • Contact information, hotline is posted outside the door.
 Safety officer, Technician Officer
18 Office, Shopping stall 01 month/times
  • Counters, tables, chairs, other items are arranged neatly, do not obstruct fire protection equipment, and arranged at least 50 cm away from electrical cabinets
  • Does not contain flammable substances
  • Electrical equipment with metal shell does not leak electricity, has a 3-pole plug or grounding.
  • Electrical cable of portable socket have 2 layers, no damaged, no connection.
  • Electrical appliances use more energy and/or generates heat such as: heaters, microwave ovens, water heaters, … must be managed and have safety warnings.
 Safety officer, Technician Officer
19 Spare keys 01 month/times
  • The key cabinets are not dented, deformed. Always locked when not in use.
  • All spare keys are available and noted in logbook when using.
  • Must be kept spare key of shopping stall, office, area for rent (telecommunication, laundry, …)
 Security
20 Storehouse of goods, material, chemical 03 months/times
  • Ventilation system operates smoothly, have no electrical equipment include attomatt, electrical cabinets, switches, sockets in warehouse. Power resource supply for ventilation system located outside the warehouse.
  • Fire alarm, auto fire extinguishing system, emergency lighting, portable fire extinguishers, warning signs are available.
  • Goods, materials must be neat and placed on shelves, at least 40 cm away from the ceiling and not cover the M&E system.
  • Chemicals with high risk of fire/explosion must be stored separately and have anti-spill pallet
  • No arrange working office in the warehouse
 Safety officier
21 Dormitory room 01 month/times (Randomly check 10 rooms)
  • Do not burn incense, candles, fire, or smoke.
  • Do not stock flammable, explosive, or prohibited substances
  • Do not cook
  • Do not block fire detectors and fire protection equipment
  • Do not self-connect electricity
  • Electrical equipment are in a safe situation
 Dormitory management officier
22 Cooking area 01 month/times
  • Industrial rice cookers and food warmers must not exhaust water, don’t have a lot of rust on the burners, and are cleaned every 2 weeks.
  • The exhaust fan is turned on when cooking and the container of oil steam is cleaned once a month
  • The kitchen staff is trained in fire safety; the chef has a professional certificate in fire protection.
 Operations officer

APPENDIX II – CONTENTS, FREQUENCY, RESPONSIBLE FOR OSH, SANITATION, SCHOOL HEALTH

(Issued in accordance with Regulation No. VU_AN02 dated June10, 2022)

No. Categories Frequency Contents Responsible
1 Elevator 01 month/times
  • The cabin has no abnormal noises, the handrails are firm, the lights and ventilation system are working properly.
  • The automatic rescue system (board system, battery, or UPS unit) maintains stable operation in case of sudden power failure, automatically drifts to the nearest floor and opens the door.
  • The elevator’s overspeed control system works stably, ensuring that when the elevator is in a free fall, the system automatically activates to help the cabin stick to the guide rail system.
  • The engine does not leak oil, the electrical cabinet has no abnormal signs.
  • The stroke limit switch at both ends of the elevator works well.
  • Door safety sensor device works normally
 Technician Officer
2 Landscaping 03 months/times
  • Have a firm support system for newly planted trees or tilted trees before the rainy season.
  • Week trees or the risk of falling or rooting trees/ branches must be found and handled timely.
 Operation officer
2 School health works 02 months/times
  • Medical equipment and tools are adequate as a prescribed list.
  • Available logbook to control medical equipment and tools.
  • Planning and following the school health care & medical plan issued.
 Medical officer
4 Waste storehouse 02 months/times
  • Drainage system is clean, not clogged, or stagnant.
  • Doors must be closed and not emit odors to the outside.
  • Waste must be classified, separated in other trash bins/zone, and available warning signs.
  • Plastic bag inside trash containers is available, except trash bigger than 240L.
 Safety officer
5 Swimming pool 01 month/times
  • Have adequate warning signs such as depth, jumping forbidden, slippery … and not blurred, lost words.
  • Have a billboard with swimming pool rules.
  • Lifeguards have rescue certificates.
  • Each swimming pool has at least 06 ring buoys and rescue poles placed in the right places. The pole is 2.5m long and painted red-white.
  • The tiles are not cracked or broken. The floor is not waterlogged or slippery.
  • The swimming pool is cleaned and treated daily
 Safety officer
6 Machinery, equipment, supplies, and substances subject to strict requirements on OSH 03 months/times
  • Equipment historical book is available and must be periodically safety technical inspection.
  • Periodically check, maintain, make, and keep safety technical records of machines, equipment, and materials according to respective national technical regulations.
  • The decision to assign managers and operators for equipment is available.
  • Rules and operating instructions are issued
  • Managers and operators have an OSH card
 Safety officer
7 Roof, ceiling, wall, floor 01 month/times
  • Ceilings, and roofs are not sagging and are waterproof
  • Walls are not cracked or broken
  • Floors, and walls are not subsided, sinked
 Technician Officer
8 Work at height (from 2 meters) Daily
  • Workers must have OSH cards for work at height and be equipped with proper PPE: seat belts, hats, shoes,…
  • There are warning signs, barriers placed at the construction area to ensure the safety of passersby.
  • Take measures to tie tools and equipment to prevent objects from falling below
  • Working floors, scaffolding, railings, and ladders.. must ensure safety before working.
 Security
9 Metal welding, cutting Daily
  • Workers must have occupational safety cards for welding and metal cutting work and be equipped with proper PPE: eye protection, shoes,…
  • Take measures to prevent welding, cutting sparks from splashing into the surrounding area
  • Cover with fireproof material or move combustible materials such as carpet, wood, paper, gasoline, oil, paint, … far away from the welding cutting position.
  • When welding, cutting in confined spaces such as tunnels, barrels, and tanks must take measures to ensure ventilation and test the concentration of oxygen, flammable, and explosive gases.
 Security
10 Electrical Installation and repair work 03 months/times
  • Worker who installs and repairs electrical equipment must have professional training and an occupational safety card for electrical work.
  • Electric wire must be installed in cable trays or fire-resistant electrical pipes, connection points must use standard bridges and connectors.
  • Electrical cabinets have warnings of electrical hazards and always locked when not in use.
  • Electrical machines and equipment must use leak-resistant devices and must be grounded.
  • Must disconnect, lock the power source and test, and ensure no power left before doing work.
 Technician Officer
11 Chemical storage and workstation 03 months/times
  • A person works with chemicals must have an occupational safety card for chemical work.
  • Chemical storage must have safety warnings, instructions for handling incidents, equipment for emergency response (sand, rags, shovels, brooms, …), and proper personal protective equipment such as boots, glasses, gloves, masks, …).
  • The ventilation system works well, the power source is connected outside of the warehouse
  • Chemicals must have chemical labels in Vietnamese and English that show the hazardous properties of the chemicals and the requirements to ensure safety; hazardous chemicals must have a chemical safety data sheet (MSDS).
  • Chemicals are stored on shelves, pallets, safely arranged, and liquid chemicals are always covered with lids.
  • It is strictly forbidden to store chemicals in food and beverage container
 Safety officer
12 Laboratory, workshop 03 months/times
  • Rules and guidelines for OSH and fire prevention are available
  • Personal protection equipment is equipped for workers.
  • Proper warning signs are easy to observe
 Safety officer
