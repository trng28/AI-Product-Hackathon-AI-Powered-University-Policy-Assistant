I. PURPOSE

VinUni is committed to providing an inclusive, equitable, and accessible learning environment for all students. This guideline aims to:

  • Ensure that students with documented or substantiated needs are provided with reasonable academic adjustments
  • Remove unnecessary barriers to learning and assessment
  • Maintain academic standards, learning outcomes, and academic integrity
  • Align with international best practices in inclusive education and student well-being

II. SCOPETop of page

This guideline applies to undergraduate and graduate students, enrolled in academic programs or activities for credit at VinUni, including those who study on a full-time basis, on exchange, or through other international agreements.

This guideline applies to all students engaged in academic activities at the university, including classes, laboratory work, clinical practice, research, examinations, fieldwork, and placements.

III. DEFINITIONTop of page

Academic Accommodation: refers to reasonable and appropriate adjustments to teaching, learning, or assessment arrangements that enable students with specific needs to access education on an equitable basis.

Academic accommodation:

  • Support access and participation
  • Do not lower academic standards
  • Do not modify essential learning outcomes, grading standards or credit requirements

Students with disabilities: Students who are individuals enrolled in the university who have a physical, sensory, cognitive, mental health, or learning impairment that substantially limits one or more major life activities, including learning, communicating, or participating in academic programs. Disabilities may be permanent, temporary, or episodic in nature. This definition aligns with applicable human rights and accessibility legislation and serves as the basis for determining eligibility for academic accommodation and support services.

IV. ELIGIBILITYTop of page

Students may request Academic Accommodation only under the following valid conditions:

Eligibility Category Examples of Conditions Acceptable Evidence
Long-term Conditions or Disabilities
  • Physical, sensory, neurological, or mobility impairments
  • Learning differences or neurodiversity
  • Chronic health conditions
  • An original medical report or clinical summary that explicitly states the professional diagnosis, supported by relevant diagnostic reports from a registered public or international hospital, ensuring all documents are officially stamped and signed by a specialist.
  • Official disability documentation from a government agency
  • For long‑term medical conditions, the medical report must be issued within the past 6 months.
Mental Health Conditions
  • Anxiety, depression, stress-related or psychological conditions
  • Other conditions verified by qualified professionals, where applicable.
  • Medical report from a hospital confirming need for academic support
  • For temporary medical conditions, the medical report must be issued within the past 30 days.
Temporary Medical Conditions
  • Serious illness, injury, surgery, or hospitalization
  • Pregnancy-related or post-partum conditions
  • Medical report specifying condition and recovery period
  • For temporary medical conditions, the medical report must be issued within the past 30 days.
Exceptional or Compassionate Circumstances
  • Bereavement
  • Significant personal or family crises
  • Other circumstances deemed reasonable by the University
  • Supporting letters from counselor, advisor, or Student Affairs officer / other supporting documents.
  • Self-declaration with contextual explanation may be accepted for short-term or urgent situations, such as bereavement, family emergencies, or sudden illness. Repeated requests may be subject to further review and additional supporting evidence.
  • Other evidence might be required if applicable

Note:

  • All documentation will be treated as strictly confidential
  • Evidence is reviewed on a case-by-case basis
  • Temporary accommodation may be approved provisionally, pending further documentation
  • Submission of false or misleading information may result in action under the Student Code of Conduct.

V. GUIDING PRINCIPLESTop of page

Equity, Not Advantage Academic accommodation ensures fair access, not academic advantage.
Reasonableness Accommodation must be reasonable, proportionate, and academically appropriate.
Confidentiality All personal, medical, and supporting information will be handled with strict confidentiality.
Individualized Assessment Requests are assessed on a case-by-case basis.
Shared Responsibility Effective accommodation requires collaboration among students, faculty, and the University.

 VI. TYPES OF ACADEMIC ACCOMMODATIONTop of page

While the below list outlines common types of academic accommodations, specific arrangements may vary depending on the student’s medical profile and learning needs. Final accommodation recommendations shall be developed by the Student Affairs Management Office, in consultation with the Registrar Office and, where necessary, the student’s treating healthcare professional or qualified specialist. The relevant academic unit shall determine how the approved accommodations are implemented while maintaining academic standards and learning outcomes.

Exam & Assessment Accommodations 1.     Extended time for tests and examinations of up to 25% of the total allotted time

2.     Separate or quiet testing environments

3.     Scheduled rest breaks during examinations

4.     Alternative examination formats (e.g., oral, large print, or electronic), where academically appropriate and without altering learning outcomes
Classroom & Instructional Accommodation 5.     Accessible seating arrangements

6.     Permission to audio-record lectures, where appropriate

7.     Provision of lecture slides or notes in advance, where feasible

8.     Reasonable flexibility in attendance requirements, for up to 20% of total class sessions.
Assignment & Coursework Accommodation 9.     Reasonable extensions of assignment deadlines, for up to 7 days per request.

10.  Alternative formats for coursework submission (e.g., oral presentation in place of written submission), provided that essential learning outcomes are met
Experiential Learning   Accommodation 11.  Reasonable modifications to tasks, schedules, or environments for internships, laboratories, or field trips

12.  Accessible transportation or logistical arrangements, where feasible and relevant

Academic Safeguard Statement: Academic accommodations shall not fundamentally alter the essential requirements, learning outcomes, or academic standards of a course or program. Decisions regarding accommodation that affect assessment methods or experiential learning components shall be made in consultation with relevant academic units.

The University reserves the right to review accommodation arrangements if there are changes in the student’s condition, academic program, or course requirements.

VII. VALIDITY PERIODTop of page

The Validity Period defines the specific timeframe during which this policy remains in effect. This section clarifies when the policy becomes active, how long it is applicable, and any conditions related to its duration.

1.     Interim academic accommodation
  • Interim accommodations are temporary measures granted solely in cases of medical urgency where immediate support is required to prevent significant barriers to learning.
  • Interim accommodation is approved for up to 02 weeks, during which students must submit the required documentation to be considered for ongoing accommodation.
  • If documentation is not provided within this period, interim accommodation will expire after 02 weeks unless agreed otherwise.
2.     Periodic academic accommodation
  • Periodic accommodation is temporary support approved for a specific academic term.
  • To maintain these accommodations, students must resubmit updated documentation each semester demonstrating continued need for support.
  • Failure to provide the latest evidence before the start of the new term may result in the accommodation being discontinued.
3.     Permanent academic accommodation
  • Permanent accommodations remain valid for the entirety of the student’s academic career at VinUniversity.
  • Students are not required to resubmit their evidence each semester to review accommodation. However, they are encouraged to schedule a meeting if there are any changes to their medical documentation or program of study.

Steps Stage Description Responsible Unit(s) Timeline/notes
1 Submission of Request – Student submits a formal Academic Accommodation Request to the Student Affairs Management Office, specifying the accommodation sought and providing all relevant supporting documentation (medical, psychological, or other substantiating information where applicable).

Contact: connect-sam@vinuni.edu.vn

*In case of mental health conditions, please refer to the Mental Health Counsellor to have a counselling consultation prior to this process.

 Student Based on individual needs

 
2 Initial Review, Eligibility Assessment and Preliminary Accommodation Recommendation – Review of request completeness, eligibility, and urgency.

– Clarification or additional information may be requested from the student if needed.

* In case of medical urgency, interim academic accommodation is immediately activated.

 Student Affairs Management (SAM) Normally within 05 working days
3 Academic Consultation (if required) – For cases involving assessments, examinations, attendance requirements, or learning outcomes, the Student Well-being Unit formally refers the case to the Registrar’s Office.

– The Registrar consults with relevant academic units (VPAA, Program Director, course coordinator, or AQA) to assess feasibility and academic alignment.

 SAM and the Registrar (REG) Conducted on a need basis
4 Decision & Approval – Faculty: Proposal of appropriate accommodation implementation based on verification from SAM & REG

– Program Director: Academic review & Approval

– Vice Provost for Academic Administration: Final Approval (if escalation needed)

 College Leaders, VPAA Normally within 03 working days from the receipt of complete documentation
5 Notification to Student – Formal written notification to the student outlining approved accommodation, duration, and any conditions attached. Registrar Normally within 24 hours from the receipt of decision
6 Notification to Faculty – Communication of approved accommodation to relevant faculty, TA members and units on a need-to-know basis, without disclosure of confidential personal or medical details. Registrar Prior to implementation
7 Implementation Faculty members implement approved accommodations in teaching, learning, or assessment activities. Faculty, TA As specified in the approval
8 Monitoring & Follow-up Monitoring effectiveness of accommodation; adjustments may be made if circumstances change or implementation challenges arise. Registrar, SAM & College On-going
9 Review or Appeal (if applicable) Students may request reconsideration if new information is available or circumstances materially change. SAM
