## Content

The purpose of this policy is to:
Outline the requirements to identify, avoid and manage real or apparent conflicts of interest when faculty and staff engage in outside paid activities such as consulting, appointments, entrepreneurial ventures, teaching, etc.
Guide the allocation and distribution of external income for faculty and staff from university supported activities such as externally funded research, additional responsibilities, and Executive Education.
